Welcome to the new home of Swansea University Esports.

The old Squarespace site has been replaced with a fast static site that the committee
can edit directly through a simple admin panel — no developer required. The site is
hosted free on Cloudflare Pages, so it costs the society nothing to keep online.

Everything here is a work in progress. If you spot something wrong, ping the committee
on Discord.
